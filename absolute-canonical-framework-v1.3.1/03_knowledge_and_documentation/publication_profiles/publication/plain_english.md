# Publication profiles, confidentiality, and claim sanitization — Plain English
This module answers **Which governed claims may this audience receive?** It applies three canonical rules: Publication views shall derive from verified internal claims through audience/classification policy, sanitization, unsupported-claim checks, rights checks, secret scanning, freshness review, and required approval.; Required publication classes shall include internal exhaustive, restricted engineering, controlled enterprise, public commercial, engineering quick reference, plain English, and machine-consumable.; Every derivative claim shall retain source claim ID, version/hash, truth state, allowed audience, sanitization policy, approval, freshness, and invalidation trigger.
It first checks identity, scope, authority, permission, freshness, and rollback; performs one bounded operation; records evidence and limitations; and reports a truthful state. The key failure is `restricted or unsupported claim enters a derivative`. The response is to stop the affected action, preserve the finding, restore a safe checkpoint where needed, fix the canonical source, and rerun affected checks. This release remains a candidate pending independent review.

# Decision, risk, assumption, unknown, and waiver control — Plain English
This module answers **Who may decide this issue, and what reopens it?** It applies three canonical rules: Material decisions, assumptions, risks, unknowns, exceptions, and waivers shall have stable IDs, owners, evidence, impact, status, review triggers, and expiry where applicable.; Owner input shall be requested only for material legal/commercial authority, credentials, recurring cost, broad permissions, external publication, destructive action, or irreversible architecture that cannot be derived safely.; Unknown or contradictory evidence shall remain visible and shall block only the affected scope unless propagation is proven.
It first checks identity, scope, authority, permission, freshness, and rollback; performs one bounded operation; records evidence and limitations; and reports a truthful state. The key failure is `unknown or waiver is converted into permanent fact`. The response is to stop the affected action, preserve the finding, restore a safe checkpoint where needed, fix the canonical source, and rerun affected checks. This release remains a candidate pending independent review.

# Scoped authority, truth states, and conflict handling — Plain English
This module answers **What is authoritative for this exact claim, scope, and version?** It applies three canonical rules: Authority shall be scoped by subject, artifact class, representation, version, lifecycle, and evidence; no file is universally authoritative.; Material claims shall use explicit truth states that distinguish verified fact, approved requirement/design, inference, plan, unknown, conflict, supersession, and rejection.; Equal-rank material conflicts shall be recorded and resolved by evidence-backed decision; vague compromise shall not replace contradictory requirements.
It first checks identity, scope, authority, permission, freshness, and rollback; performs one bounded operation; records evidence and limitations; and reports a truthful state. The key failure is `two active authorities compete silently`. The response is to stop the affected action, preserve the finding, restore a safe checkpoint where needed, fix the canonical source, and rerun affected checks. This release remains a candidate pending independent review.

# Fresh and adversarial independent verification — Plain English
This module answers **Has a fresh verifier tried to disprove this exact hash?** It applies three canonical rules: Final verification shall use a fresh context/role and preferably another provider with direct access to the frozen candidate, original mission, requirements, raw evidence, tests, conflicts, and hashes.; The verifier shall attempt to disprove readiness, and repairs shall invalidate affected assurance and require transitive revalidation.; A final certified status shall name the exact candidate hash; when independent verification is unavailable, status shall remain release-candidate verification-pending or unverifiable-blocked.
It first checks identity, scope, authority, permission, freshness, and rollback; performs one bounded operation; records evidence and limitations; and reports a truthful state. The key failure is `builder self-certifies or review applies to another hash`. The response is to stop the affected action, preserve the finding, restore a safe checkpoint where needed, fix the canonical source, and rerun affected checks.

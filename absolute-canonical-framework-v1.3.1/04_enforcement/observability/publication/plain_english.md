# Observability, error taxonomy, incidents, and fault-seeking health — Plain English
This module answers **What was actually tested, when, and what remains unknown?** It applies three canonical rules: Observability shall expose state, health, errors, dependencies, freshness, and evidence without treating absence of alerts as health.; Errors and incidents shall use stable taxonomy, severity, scope, root-cause state, containment, recovery, evidence, and follow-up records.; Watchdogs and health reporters shall be tested with fault injection and shall fail truthfully when dependencies or their own reporting path are unavailable.
It first checks identity, scope, authority, permission, freshness, and rollback; performs one bounded operation; records evidence and limitations; and reports a truthful state. The key failure is `silence or stale probe is labeled healthy`. The response is to stop the affected action, preserve the finding, restore a safe checkpoint where needed, fix the canonical source, and rerun affected checks. This release remains a candidate pending independent review.

# Source admission, lineage, no-loss reconciliation, and supersession — Plain English
This module answers **Which concepts survive each source, why, and where is every disposition?** It applies three canonical rules: Source admission shall preserve raw bytes, hashes, collection boundaries, provenance, rights, confidentiality, extraction diagnostics, lineage, and explicit artifact disposition.; Reconciliation shall compare concepts and structured critical differences, preserve unique information, separate candidate discovery from replacement decisions, and build a shadow candidate before promotion.; Automatic high-impact replacement shall require complete provenance, rights, no unique knowledge loss, no unmapped links, no critical contradiction, calibrated precision, and rollback.
It first checks identity, scope, authority, permission, freshness, and rollback; performs one bounded operation; records evidence and limitations; and reports a truthful state. The key failure is `unique meaning is silently lost or filename becomes authority`. The response is to stop the affected action, preserve the finding, restore a safe checkpoint where needed, fix the canonical source, and rerun affected checks. This release remains a candidate pending independent review.

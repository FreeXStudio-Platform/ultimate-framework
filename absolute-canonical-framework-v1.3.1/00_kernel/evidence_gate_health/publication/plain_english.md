# Evidence, gates, health, and completion claims — Plain English
This module answers **What exact evidence supports this status and candidate hash?** It applies three canonical rules: PASS, COMPLETE, VERIFIED, HEALTHY, INSTALLED, DEPLOYED, and RELEASED claims shall cite current evidence bound to exact scope and candidate identity.; Canonical gate states shall be PASS, FAIL, BLOCKED, NOT_RUN, INCONCLUSIVE, STALE, INVALID_REPORT, or WAIVED_WITH_APPROVED_EXPIRY; legacy accepted-risk states migrate to PASS plus an explicit risk/waiver record.; Health shall distinguish healthy, degraded, partial, dependency-failed, unknown, untested, stale, misconfigured, and root-cause-unknown, and shall test the reporter itself.
It first checks identity, scope, authority, permission, freshness, and rollback; performs one bounded operation; records evidence and limitations; and reports a truthful state. The key failure is `PASS is issued without evidence`. The response is to stop the affected action, preserve the finding, restore a safe checkpoint where needed, fix the canonical source, and rerun affected checks. This release remains a candidate pending independent review.

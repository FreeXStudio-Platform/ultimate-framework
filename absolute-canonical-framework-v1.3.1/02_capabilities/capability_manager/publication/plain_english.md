# Capability selection, health, permission, and fallback — Plain English
This module answers **What is the smallest healthy and permitted capability set?** It applies three canonical rules: Every executable or context-providing capability shall have a registry record covering identity, version, provenance, triggers, permissions, data scope, cost, health, fallback, security/license review, and lifecycle.; The active packet shall use the smallest sufficient set of healthy, authorized, attributable capabilities and shall prefer deterministic/local methods for mechanical work.; Unavailable, degraded, unauthenticated, or over-privileged capabilities shall not be represented as usable; write/external/destructive actions remain approval-bounded.
It first checks identity, scope, authority, permission, freshness, and rollback; performs one bounded operation; records evidence and limitations; and reports a truthful state. The key failure is `enabled capability has unknown health or excessive permission`. The response is to stop the affected action, preserve the finding, restore a safe checkpoint where needed, fix the canonical source, and rerun affected checks. This release remains a candidate pending independent review.

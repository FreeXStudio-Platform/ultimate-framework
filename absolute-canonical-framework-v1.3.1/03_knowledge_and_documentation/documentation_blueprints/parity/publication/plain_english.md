# Narrative, blueprint, machine, visual, and test parity — Plain English
This module answers **Do all applicable representations agree on behavior and risk?** It applies three canonical rules: Narrative, blueprint, machine, visual, test, and publication representations shall share stable scope IDs while retaining form-specific authority.; Modules shall be authored section-by-section in lockstep when representations apply, and substantive change shall invalidate and regenerate affected forms.; Parity validation shall cover identity, requirements, terminology, states, interfaces, permissions, failure/recovery, dependencies, visuals, publication, versions, hashes, and tests.
It first checks identity, scope, authority, permission, freshness, and rollback; performs one bounded operation; records evidence and limitations; and reports a truthful state. The key failure is `narrative, machine, visual, or test meaning drifts`. The response is to stop the affected action, preserve the finding, restore a safe checkpoint where needed, fix the canonical source, and rerun affected checks. This release remains a candidate pending independent review.

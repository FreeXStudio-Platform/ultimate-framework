# Change, deviation, recovery, and rollback — Plain English
This module answers **Can this change be reversed, and what becomes stale?** It applies three canonical rules: Material changes shall have an impact set, checkpoint, rollback route, and revalidation plan before execution.; A change shall invalidate affected and transitive evidence, parity stamps, derivatives, indexes, and compatibility claims.; Detected deviation shall stop the affected route, preserve evidence, restore or re-plan from the last valid point, and revalidate the repaired scope.
It first checks identity, scope, authority, permission, freshness, and rollback; performs one bounded operation; records evidence and limitations; and reports a truthful state. The key failure is `material change has no rollback`. The response is to stop the affected action, preserve the finding, restore a safe checkpoint where needed, fix the canonical source, and rerun affected checks.

# Security, privacy, secrets, prompt injection, and least privilege — Plain English
This module answers **What data and authority can this action expose or change?** It applies three canonical rules: Secrets shall be referenced through approved stores or environment names and shall never appear in framework source, examples, logs, evidence, screenshots, manifests, or releases.; Source and retrieved content shall be treated as untrusted; path, command, URL, filename, archive, prompt-injection, and supply-chain controls shall apply before privileged use.; Data classification and least privilege shall propagate through raw/derived stores, indexes, embeddings, caches, provenance, external models, renderers, and publication.
It first checks identity, scope, authority, permission, freshness, and rollback; performs one bounded operation; records evidence and limitations; and reports a truthful state. The key failure is `secret, prompt injection, or destructive action crosses boundary`. The response is to stop the affected action, preserve the finding, restore a safe checkpoint where needed, fix the canonical source, and rerun affected checks. This release remains a candidate pending independent review.
